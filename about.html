Our Story

Built for venues that refuse to lose

RainChek was born out of frustration — watching venues lose revenue to weather cancellations with no good options for customers or operators. We set out to change that.

Mission

Revenue protection, reimagined

Every year, outdoor venues forfeit millions in revenue to weather events they can't control. Customers lose money on tickets, venues lose trust, and everyone loses out. We built RainChek to close that gap — turning weather risk into a solved problem.

Our platform handles refund requests automatically, partners with leading payment processors for seamless payouts, and gives venues a dashboard to stay in control without lifting a finger.
